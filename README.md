## 💡 About BudgetWise

**BudgetWise** is a personal finance app designed to make managing your money simple and intuitive.

Instead of overwhelming you with complicated spreadsheets and financial jargon, BudgetWise gives you a clear view of where your money is going, helping you make better spending decisions.

### Built for

* 💰 People who want to track their spending
* 📊 Anyone looking for a simple budgeting system
* 🎓 Students managing their finances
* 🧑‍💼 Professionals looking to stay on top of expenses
* 🎯 Anyone working toward better financial habits

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Understand your spending

Get a clear overview of your income, expenses, and spending patterns.

### 💳 Track expenses

Quickly record your expenses and keep your financial activity organized.

### 🎯 Set financial goals

Create goals and keep track of your progress toward them.

### 📈 Visualize your finances

Turn your financial data into simple, understandable insights.

### ⚡ Designed for simplicity

Budgeting shouldn't feel like accounting. BudgetWise focuses on making everyday money management fast and effortless.
